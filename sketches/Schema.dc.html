Reading it
Each row is column, then its type, then whether it may be null.
owns it, cascades on delete
points at it, may be null
All ids are uuid with a random default. cook_notes also points at steps and cooks; those two lines are left off so the picture stays readable.
recipes is the hub. Ingredients, equipment, steps, cooks and notes all hang off it.
steps nests into itself. A tree, not a graph, so no cycle can exist. One level only, enforced in code.
No foreign key stops a step in recipe A linking an ingredient in recipe B. Checked on write, in the same transaction.
Saving a shared recipe copies it. No shared rows, so one user still owns everything. cooks and cook_notes are never copied.
