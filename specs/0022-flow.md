# 0022: Flow

**Status:** Draft
**Depends on:** 0008, 0020, 0021

## Context

0008 let an author nest a step while writing it: every step card carried "Add a step to do
meanwhile", "Make this a main step" and "Do this during the previous step". Writing a recipe and
organising it are different jobs, and doing both on one card made every card heavier and the
structure hard to see. Clicking through after 0021 made that plain: you cannot tell what happens
during what without reading every card.

## Goal

Steps are written as a flat list, and a separate Flow view says what happens during what and
shows the result as a picture.

## Out of scope

- Any change to the API or the tables. `parentStepId` already stores exactly this; only the editor
  changes.
- The recipe screen. It keeps the indented "Meanwhile" list from 0008. The flow chart is for
  organising, and reading it while cooking is 0012's problem.
- A second level. A step that has parallel steps under it cannot itself be made parallel to
  another, as the data model rules in `CLAUDE.md` require.
- Suggesting what could run in parallel. The author decides, per `CLAUDE.md`; the app only draws
  what they decided.
- A title field on steps. The Flow view names a step by the start of its instruction. A separate
  title would be a second thing to write for every step, for the benefit of one view.
- Dragging boxes around the chart. Parallel steps are chosen from a list and undone with a button.
- Timing in the chart: no bars sized by duration, no critical path. A box and a branch is all.

## UI

**Steps page and the steps part of the edit screen.** A flat list of every step, in reading order:
main steps in order, and a step that runs during another right after the step it runs during. Each
card has instruction, time, links, note, and the move buttons from 0021. A card whose step runs
during another says so under its number, "During step 2", as information, not a control. The
nest, promote and "Add a step to do meanwhile" buttons are gone. Moving a card moves it within its
own level: a main step among main steps, a parallel step among its siblings. Removing a step that
has parallel steps makes them main steps in its place, as the server would.

**Flow page, `(app)/recipes/[id]/flow`, the fourth page of the create flow.** After "Steps" and
before "Review". Two parts.

The first is the list: each main step as one line, numbered, named by the start of its instruction,
with a button "Add steps to do meanwhile". Pressing it opens a checklist of every other step that
could run during this one: any other main step that has no parallel steps of its own. Ticking one
moves it under this step; the list and the chart update at once. Under each main step, its parallel
steps are listed as "2a", "2b", each with a button "Back to the main flow", which returns it to the
main list just after its former parent. A recipe with fewer than two steps says there is nothing to
organise yet.

The second is the overview: a chart drawn with ordinary views, no library. Main steps run down the
page as numbered boxes joined by a line. A step with parallel steps has a branch to its side
carrying their boxes, joined back to the line below the step, so "these happen during that" is
visible at a glance. The chart is one element to a screen reader per step, reading "Step 2, roast
the beetroot, meanwhile 2a chop the dill, 2b boil the eggs".

Done saves the steps with their new structure in one `PATCH` and goes on to the review. Skip goes
to the review without saving. Offline, Done fails at once with the offline message and keeps the
structure on screen.

**Edit screen.** Below the steps, a "Flow" section holding the same list and chart, editing the same
draft as the steps above it, so a change on either side shows on both. One Save, as before.

**Components**, each in its own folder with styles, test and index: the flow editor (the list with
its buttons), the step picker (the checklist, in a modal like the unit picker), and the flow chart.
Each gets a gallery entry; the chart's shows a recipe with and without parallel steps.

All new strings go through `t()`, in English and Latvian.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint` and `pnpm test` pass across all three workspaces.
- [ ] Ticking a step in the picker moves it under the chosen step and it leaves the main list;
      "Back to the main flow" returns it just after its former parent, asserted in a test.
- [ ] A step that has parallel steps is not offered in any picker, and a step is never offered to
      itself, asserted in a test.
- [ ] Removing a main step with parallel steps in the steps editor makes them main steps in its
      place, asserted in a test.
- [ ] The draft sent on Done nests exactly what the Flow view shows, asserted in a test on the
      helper that builds the body.
- [ ] The chart reads each main step as one element naming its parallel steps, asserted in a
      component test, and shows a branch only for steps that have them.
- [ ] On the simulator: write three steps, open Flow, tick step 3 as meanwhile for step 2, see the
      chart branch, press Done, and the recipe screen shows step 3 as "2a, during step 2".
- [ ] The steps editor shows no nest or promote buttons, and a parallel step shows "During step
      N" under its number, asserted in a test.
- [ ] The Latvian file lists every new key.
- _Manual follow-up, not a gate:_ a VoiceOver or TalkBack walkthrough of the Flow page, checking
  the picker's checkboxes announce their state and the chart reads one element per main step.
